{%- comment -%} Renders a post title written as: Plain Title | 🔥 [Hacker News](https://...) Left of the first " | " is the post title (escaped, linked to the post). Everything after it is rendered as markdown, so links work. Usage: {% include post_title.html post=post %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign parts = include.post.title | split: " | " -%} {%- assign main_title = parts[0] | strip -%} {{ main_title | escape }} {%- if parts.size > 1 -%} {%- assign extra = parts | shift | join: " | " | markdownify | strip | remove: "

" | remove: "

" -%} | {{ extra }} {%- endif -%}
